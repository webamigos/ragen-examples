Acme Cloud — Billing and refunds

Last updated 12 March. Applies to all Acme Cloud plans except Enterprise, which is governed by its own contract.

Billing cycle

Acme Cloud bills monthly in arrears, on the same day of the month as your signup date. If your signup day does not exist in a given month (e.g. the 31st in February), you are billed on the last day of that month instead. Invoices are issued in EUR and include VAT where applicable.

Plan changes

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remainder of the cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next cycle — you keep the higher plan's limits until then, and no credit is issued for the unused portion.

Refunds

A first subscription can be refunded in full within 14 days of the initial charge, provided the account used under 10% of its included seat-hours. After 14 days, refunds are issued as account credit rather than to the original payment method.

Overage charges are not refundable once the cycle has closed, because the underlying capacity was already reserved. If overage was caused by an incident on our side, open a support ticket and we will waive it as a one-off credit.

Failed payments

A failed charge is retried after 3, 5 and 7 days. After the third failure the workspace moves to read-only: existing deployments keep serving traffic, but no new deployments can be created. Data is retained for 30 days from that point, and deleted afterwards.

Cancellation

Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current cycle runs to its end. Export your data before the cycle closes — after cancellation the export API is available for 30 days, then the workspace is purged.
